What is Research Methodology?

Research methodology is the systematic approach used to collect, analyze, and interpret data in academic and professional research. It forms the foundation of credible and reliable research outcomes.

Key Components:

  • Research design and framework
  • Data collection methods
  • Sampling techniques
  • Data analysis procedures
  • Quality assurance measures
